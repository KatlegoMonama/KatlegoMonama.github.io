Seasonal Analysis (12-Month Trend) Correlation of Gold & DXY Dollar

Download GITHUB SLIDE DECK
Q1 (January – March)
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ BEST QUARTER
Historically the strongest quarter for gold. January is frequently the best-performing month due to:
Chinese New Year demand. Institutional portfolio reallocation.
New-year investment inflows. Studies of long-term seasonality consistently identify January as one of gold's strongest months.
Trading Bias: Bullish

Q2 (April – June)
⭐⭐⭐ Moderate
Characteristics: Consolidation phase. Profit-taking after Q1 rally.
Mixed performance. April often remains supportive,
but June is frequently among the weakest months historically.
Trading Bias: Range-bound

Q3 (July – September)
⭐⭐ Weakest Quarter
Characteristics: Summer liquidity decline. Reduced institutional participation.
Higher probability of false breakouts.
Multiple seasonality studies identify July and September among the weakest months for gold performance.
Trading Bias: Cautious

Q4 (October – December)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ Strong
Drivers: Indian wedding season demand, Central bank reserve accumulation, Year-end portfolio rebalancing.
Historically Q4 ranks as the second-strongest quarter after Q1.
Trading Bias: Bullish
